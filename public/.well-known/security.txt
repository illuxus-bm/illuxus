# When reporting a vulnerability, please include:
#  - A clear description of the issue and affected URL / endpoint
#  - Step-by-step reproduction (curl, screenshots, or a short PoC)
#  - The impact you believe the bug could have
#  - Whether you would like public credit and under what handle
#
# We aim to:
#  - Acknowledge new reports within 24 business hours (Mon–Fri, IST)
#  - Provide a triage outcome within 5 business days
#  - Resolve confirmed critical issues within 30 days
#
# Please do NOT:
#  - Run scans that materially degrade service for other customers
#  - Access, modify, or exfiltrate data that does not belong to you
#  - Publicly disclose a vulnerability before we have shipped a fix
#  - Conduct social engineering against illuxus staff or customers
#
# Out of scope:
#  - Reports based purely on missing security headers without exploitation
#  - Theoretical attacks without a working proof of concept
#  - Findings on third-party services we embed (Stripe, Razorpay, LiveKit, etc.)
#    — please report those to the respective vendor directly
#  - Denial of service via volumetric traffic
